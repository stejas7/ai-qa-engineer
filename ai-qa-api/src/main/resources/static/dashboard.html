Purpose & Vision

Scorpion is an Autonomous AI QA Engineer designed to take end-to-end ownership of software quality engineering activities traditionally performed through dedicated QA execution roles. Its long-term objective is to enable engineering organizations to operate with minimal dependency on dedicated manual QA execution by autonomously understanding requirements, designing tests, generating automation, executing UAT, diagnosing failures, self-healing eligible automation issues, validating regression and producing evidence-backed quality decisions.

Scorpion acts as an autonomous QA engineer within the software delivery lifecycle — continuously understanding, testing, validating, diagnosing and protecting product quality with minimal human intervention.
